the depth of the prior watchlist expansion) would mean ~3,500-4,500 handles total — a multi-day
effort across many sequential subagent-research waves, not a single pass.

debate cluster (sections 1-2, ~45 micro-niches) plus a broad sampling of 8-10 other major
categories (paranormal, true crime, history, finance, business, science, politics, true-crime,
